3-Step Universal Identification Strategy
  1. Locate the North Rotational Axis Pole.
  2. Observe if the North Pole leans TOWARD or AWAY from the Sun.
  3. Toward = Northern Summer | Away = Northern Winter (Equinoxes lean sideways).
